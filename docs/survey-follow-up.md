# Survey follow-up test

Run this with the original survey participants after the UX update.

## Tasks

1. Open the welcome guidance and select a profile relevant to your work.
2. Find one useful article, save it, and mark another article as read.
3. Mark one clearly irrelevant article as noise and check that it disappears.
4. Change the period to the last 7 days, then return to 90 days.

## Questions

Ask each participant to rate, from 1 to 5:

- I understood what the feed is for and where to start.
- The feed responded quickly enough while I changed profile or filters.
- It was clear how to save, read, and hide articles.
- The time-period selector gave me enough control.
- Overall experience.

Record one concrete false positive and one concrete missing article where possible. Use the article title, source, expected profile, and a one-sentence reason. These examples are the input for the next source or classification adjustment.
